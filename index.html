How Version 2.1 evaluates a college: Hard constraints override everything else. After that, the app gives greatest weight to admissions evidence (pre-read and confirmed support), then recruiting evidence, program need, athletic fit, academic fit, and finally student priorities. Lower-level positive signals cannot mathematically cancel a decisive higher-level negative signal.

Questions to Ask Coaches

  1. Where do I currently stand on your recruiting list?
  2. How many athletes are you recruiting in my position/event this year?
  3. How many do you expect to support in admissions?
  4. Would you request a pre-read for me, and when?
  5. If the pre-read is positive, what level of support should I expect?
  6. Is support contingent on Early Decision?
  7. If I am not supported, is there still a realistic roster or walk-on path?

Counselor Notes

Planning aid only; not a guarantee of admission, roster placement, coach support, playing time, or aid.
